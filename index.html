Wesley Deng

I am a financial economist at UNSW Sydney. My research focuses on two areas: the real effects of financial market frictions on the real economy, and the interactions between human capital and innovation. I also explore other questions facing firms and financial markets using big data and AI analytics.

My research interests
Corporate finance; the real effects of financial market frictions; the economics of innovation; labor and finance; empirical asset pricing; AI and finance.
